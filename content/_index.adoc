Dr. Farah Aldali is a Syrian dentist now living in Germany, where she is working toward full licensure.
She trained at Al Andalus University for Medical Sciences, ran her own clinic in Tartous for nearly two years, and spent a year providing specialised skincare treatments before moving abroad.
On Instagram she shares dentistry, skincare, and everyday life in Germany.
