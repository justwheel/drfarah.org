From March to May 2026, I completed a full-time preparation course at the [Freiburg International Academy][1] (FIA) in Germany, covering the treatment systems and core disciplines of German dental practice — pediatric dentistry, oral surgery, prosthodontics, and endodontics — with hands-on clinical training under faculty supervision.

The course gave me the foundation to confidently pursue the *Kenntnisprüfung*, Germany's dental knowledge exam and the key step toward practicing dentistry there.
