From March to May 2026, I completed a full-time preparation course at the [Freiburg International Academy][1] (FIA) in Germany, working toward the *Kenntnisprüfung* — Germany's dental knowledge exam required to practice dentistry there.
The curriculum covered the treatment categories recognized by German health insurance — *Regelversorgung* (standard care), *gleichartige Versorgung* (comparable care), and *andersartige Versorgung* (alternative care) — along with pediatric dentistry, oral surgery, prosthodontics, and endodontics, plus hands-on training under faculty supervision.

The course gave me the foundation to confidently pursue the *Kenntnisprüfung* and a deeper understanding of Germany's healthcare and health-insurance systems — the key step toward practicing dentistry there.
